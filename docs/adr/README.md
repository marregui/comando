# Decision records

One record per decision: its context, the decision and its consequences, the date, and who decided. Records 1 to 16 are
decisions D1 to D16 of the design plan of 2026-10-12, which is kept outside this repository. Later records come from the
implementation and from the author's daily use.

A decision is changed by a later record that names the one it replaces or amends in its context, and the earlier
record's status names the later one; a small change is a dated paragraph under the record's Amendments. Every record
has the same shape:

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| header | date, status (accepted, proposed, replaced or amended by a later record), who decided |
| 1. Context | the problem and the facts that forced a decision, with numbers where measured |
| 2. Decision | the rule, or a table of rules with the reason for each; who decided what |
| 3. Consequences | what changed in the code, what it costs, what it rules out |
| 4. Alternatives | the options rejected and why, where the record names them |
